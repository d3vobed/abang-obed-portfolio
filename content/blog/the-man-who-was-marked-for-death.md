Hemingway wrote that every man is broken by something, and that a man can be destroyed but not defeated. We live most of our lives as if time is a warehouse we can withdraw from later. The truth is closer to the opposite: each morning hands us another chance to shift the script.

Being "marked for death" is just another way of saying *finite*. Everyone carries it. The difference between dread and freedom is whether we read that mark as a sentence or as an invitation.

I have spent years in security work, where the question is always *what breaks first?* Systems, like people, fail quietly until they don't. The discipline is in noticing the small fractures early, and in choosing, deliberately, what to reinforce.

So this is a note to myself as much as to you: stop treating the clock as an enemy. Be marked for opportunity instead.
